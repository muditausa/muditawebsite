Annual Supporters Summit

This special evening will showcase some of the exceptional music we have created, featuring live performances and a presentation on the state of our organization. This event is also an opportunity to build meaningful connections within our community, allowing you to get to know us better. We hope this event will demonstrate the quality of our work and the significant impact your support has on our mission.

23 April 2025

Mudita at the Movies

This unique collaborative concert pairs five original short films by local filmmakers with brand-new musical scores composed by our talented Resident Mudita Composers.
These scores will be brought to life by the Mudita Collective Orchestra, performing live as the films are screened for the very first time, creating an immersive fusion of story and sound.
This new concert series represents our commitment to branching out into new artistic genres and fostering meaningful connections among local artists. It’s a true celebration of cross-cultural and cross-genre collaboration—a cornerstone of our mission.

February 2025

Songs for Love:
Songwriters in the Round with Orchestra

Live at Timucua / International Chamber Music Festival / Folk / World Music Series: The third installment of Song Stringer x Mudita America's collaborative concert series, Songwriters in the Round with the Mudita Orchestra! This concert will feature the original music of Hannah Stokes, Jordan Foley, and Kaleigh Baker, prominent singer-songwriters from the Central Florida scene in a songwriters round format accompanied by the Mudita Orchestra performing custom arrangements by local composers. It will also feature some of the standard orchestra repertoire to make orchestral music more accessible while championing the voices of local songwriters.

September 14, 2024

UNION: A Cross-Cultural Collaborative Concert

The second concert in our yearly series UNION, where musician from different cultures bring together their unique artistics roots to perform traditional music and explore these elements through group improvisation. This year's performers include Tanuja Krishna, Marcinko Orozco, Eric Rokni, and Ryo Hasegawa, and will include a pre-concert discussion about their musical upbringings and how they engage in the world through their music. The concert is on Saturday, September 14, with the talk starting at 7:30pm, and the show at 8:00pm. It is located at Community Center 729, at 729 Thornton Avenue Orlando, FL 32803. December 2022        

Holiday Sounds from Around the World

Join us and celebrate our holiday season with holiday music from around the world!
This concert will feature arrangements of traditional holiday music from countries around the globe. These cultures include those from Singapore, South Korea, Venezuela, Brazil, Lebanon, Serbia, Japan, Ashkenazi, Russia, and Nigeria. Local young musicians will be joined by Mudita artists and composers from around the nation and around the world to make music together in Central Florida.

August 2023       

Songs for Summer:
Songwriters in the Round with Orchestra

SongStringer and Mudita America explore the new form of the concert experience - cross-genre collaboration. We deliver a new style of music making with local musicians, creators, and artists and create the opportunities for the community members to meet new music, new art, and new people. This concert features some of the standard orchestra repertoire to newly written charts by Hannah Stokes, Ben Wozniak, and Jan LaVigne arranged for the Mudita Collective Orchestra.

November 2023

Union

Mudita America brings four prominent artists of today to Orlando for this cultural exploration who will bring their unique artistic output to merge with others. Marin Naruse, a Japanese traditional singer and Sanshin player (uta-sha) from Amami Oshima island, Japan, Eric Rokni, a cellist from Jewish and Persian cultures, Tanuja Krishna, Indian classical dancer, Marcinko Orozco, a hip-hop influenced keyboard player will showcase an extraordinary story of oppression and unification through their performance in a collaborative setting.
